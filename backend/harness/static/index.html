What is real? What is simulated?

Roads and building footprints: OpenStreetMap. Traffic demand, driver behaviour and fixed signal plans: simulation assumptions. Building heights may be inferred. This is a SUMO harness, not a calibrated model of Indore traffic.

Fleet shares are sampling weights. Rain and obstruction are scripted speed restrictions from 01:00 to 06:00. CO₂ uses SUMO default emission classes, including unvalidated rickshaw proxies.

The rendered vehicles are simulation truth. They are not automatically phone observations.
